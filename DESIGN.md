# RAK 4 CREATIVE - Brand & Design Direction

## Identity & Purpose
- **Brand:** RAK 4 CREATIVE (`RAK4Creative`)
- **Positioning:** Full-service global creative, brand identity, digital product engineering, and performance marketing agency with headquarters in Beirut and Toronto.
- **Audience:** Enterprise marketing leaders, founders, brand directors, and internal agency operators managing content, marketing pixels, incoming leads, and email routing.
- **Dial:** `ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2` (Public Marketing Site) | `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1` (CMS Operations & Admin Workspace)

## Color System (2 Core Neutrals + 1 Brand Accent)
- **Canvas Dark (`rak-slate-950`):** `#07080B` (Primary dark background)
- **Surface Dark (`rak-slate-900`):** `#0F172A` / `#121621` (Elevated cards and workspace panels)
- **Canvas Light (`rak-slate-50`):** `#F8FAFC` (Primary light background)
- **Surface Light:** `#FFFFFF` (Light mode cards and workspace panels)
- **Primary Brand Accent (`rak-magenta`):** `#EC008C` (Hover `#C80074`, Light `#FF33AA`), used deliberately for primary CTAs, active states, and focal highlights.
- **Semantic Status Colors (Functional Only):**
  - Emerald (`#10B981`) for verified/live states
  - Amber (`#F59E0B`) for pending/warning states
  - Rose (`#F43F5E`) for errors/destructive actions

## Typography
- **Headings (`font-heading`):** `Eurostile` (with `Impact` / `Gilroy` fallback), wide geometric agency display voice (`letter-spacing: 0.045em`).
- **Body & UI Controls (`font-sans`):** `Gilroy`, clean geometric sans-serif for high legibility across long-form editorial, forms, and dense CMS tables.
- **Arabic Localization (`font-janna`):** `Janna LT` for Arabic UI notices and localized copy.

## Written Reasons for Core Visual Decisions (R-31)
- **Palette (`#07080B` / `#F8FAFC` + `#EC008C`):** High-contrast obsidian and slate surfaces let portfolio imagery and the signature `#EC008C` magenta accent command immediate visual hierarchy without competing colors.
- **Typography (`Eurostile` + `Gilroy`):** Eurostile's wide industrial geometry anchors headlines with unmistakable agency character, while Gilroy keeps body copy and data tables effortless to scan.
- **Theme Support:** Both Dark and Light modes are first-class across the public site and CMS workspace so operators and visitors can work in their preferred environment.
- **CMS Layout Architecture:** Organized around the five concrete operational workflows (Content Studio, Pixels & Tracking, Leads CRM, Email & SMTP, and Pre-Launch Audit) rather than decorative charts or fabricated metrics.
